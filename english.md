## External funds

## ACT-X Research Achievements

1. **DCC 2026: Development of a Dynamic r-Index**
    - We developed a dynamic compressed index that supports efficient text updates and fast pattern matching while using the same asymptotic space as the original r-index. We also demonstrated its practicality through experiments on large-scale datasets.
    - [[Paper]](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11510367), [[Code]](https://github.com/TNishimoto/dynamic_r_index)

2. **ESA 2026: Development of a Dynamic Compressed Index in δ-Optimal Space**
    - We developed a dynamic compressed index that supports efficient text updates and fast pattern matching in expected δ-optimal space. Experiments on large-scale datasets demonstrated its space efficiency and fast performance.
    - [[Paper]](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.ESA.2026.6), [[Code]](https://github.com/TNishimoto/dynamic-rlslp-index)
